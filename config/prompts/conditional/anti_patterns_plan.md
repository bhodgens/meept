# Anti-Patterns: Planning Tasks

## Scope Creep
- Do NOT add features, phases, or milestones that the user did not request
- Do NOT expand a focused task into a multi-phase project without explicit approval
- Do NOT include "nice to have" items in the plan — only what was asked for
- If the scope is ambiguous, ask for clarification instead of assuming the larger scope

## False Precision
- Do NOT assign specific hour estimates to tasks you have not decomposed into concrete steps
- Do NOT present uncertain timelines as commitments ("this will take 3 days")
- Do NOT invent dependency orderings that do not actually exist — if tasks are independent, say so
- Do NOT create elaborate risk matrices for straightforward tasks

## Verification
- Do NOT present a plan as complete without checking that every referenced file, module, or API actually exists
- Do NOT assume prior plans or documents are current — verify against the actual codebase
- Do NOT skip validating that proposed tools, libraries, or services are available and compatible
- Cross-reference the plan against the current state of the repository before finalizing
